S-14 StopBar
The season as a submenu. A World Tour season has more stops than a menu can hold — seventeen Masters and twenty-two Challengers — and listed flat they are an alphabet. Listed in season order the same strip becomes a submenu and a progress bar: where the tour has been, where it is tonight, where it goes next. Three states carry it. Past recedes, the live stop is the only white one and the only one that carries a dot, and the stops still to come stay legible. There are no dates on it: a date column would make it a calendar, and S-07 SeasonCalendar is the calendar. The dot is el-05's dot, so it bounces the same way, and red appears here for the same single reason it appears anywhere in this system — LIVE.

el-31 MeterBar
One value against a track, with an optional label and figure above it. S-09 Overview has a season-progress bar built into the module and el-22 CarouselIndicator has a bar that fills, but neither can be stacked to compare rows — which is what a "who is in the twelve" board needs. Track and fill only, no ticks and no scale: the figure above the bar is the number, the bar is the shape of it. The fill is --action-default, because a quantity is not a status.

el-32 VideoThumb
There is no video player in this system on purpose: ctl-07 WatchLive sends people to YouTube rather than embedding one. A thumbnail that links out is a different object, and the World Tour needed it — the channel posts vertical Shorts and a page of them is a section, not a link. Poster, format badge, play control, title. Portrait is the default because that is the shape the footage arrives in; landscape is the 16:9 variant for a recap or a full game.
