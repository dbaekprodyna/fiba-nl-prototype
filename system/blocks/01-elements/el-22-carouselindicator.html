el-22 CarouselIndicator
Position and progress for any carousel. Bars are the default because they double as a progress track; round dots are the compact variant for tight placements.
bar — position only
bar — auto-advance, current slide filling
bar — first slide
dot — compact variant
Note
Spacing between marks is 4px, half of what a chip row uses, so the set reads as one control rather than a row of separate objects. The bar is 36 x 4 and carries the corner cut like everything else.
