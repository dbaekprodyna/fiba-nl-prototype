The federation cell is one el-13 FederationTag instance in plain mode, so the flag arrives with the 2px surface-grey halo already applied. On a hover row the halo and the row share the same grey and it disappears — by then the white in the flag is sitting on grey and separates on its own. Terminology is fixed: "Federation", never country or nation. Conference names carry the age category, so there is no separate U23 / U21 column, and no point difference anywhere.
