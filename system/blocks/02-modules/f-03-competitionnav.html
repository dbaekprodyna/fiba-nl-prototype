Note
Logos are the real assets from the SVG folder: 3x3-logo.svg in the lozenge and 3x3NL-Logo.svg as the wordmark. Two content decisions are visible: Qualification sits where Standings used to (LP-11), and About is a nav item (LP-03). Home is carried by the logo, which is the pattern users have been trained on for two decades and expect on desktop; a separate Home item would be redundant there. On mobile the bottom bar carries an explicit Home tab, because that is the established convention on both iOS and Android and a small logo is a poor touch target. Both platforms keep the logo linking home, so the behaviour is consistent even though the affordance differs.
