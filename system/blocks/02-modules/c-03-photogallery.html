Note
The viewer opens on the whole set and drills into a single photo on tap, which is how Google Photos and Apple Photos behave and is the right order here: a stop produces a few hundred frames and a fan wants to scan before committing to one. Position and progress are el-22 CarouselIndicator. The viewer surface is light like the rest of the system rather than a black theatre.
