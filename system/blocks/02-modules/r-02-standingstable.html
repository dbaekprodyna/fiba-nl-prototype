Note
Two views, and the difference between them is deliberate. Default lists the twelve federations that are qualified or shortlisted, in alphabetical order and with no position column — because a rank next to an alphabetical list invites the wrong reading. Expanded is the click-through: the full field with a position column, sortable on every numeric header. Hiding two columns in the third state changed nothing except two booleans; no frames were moved and no widths re-measured.
